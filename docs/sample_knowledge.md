# Tech Consultation Clinic - Services & FAQs

## About Us
Welcome to the Tech Consultation Clinic! We offer professional IT and technical support for individuals and small businesses. Our operating hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

2. **Network Setup & Security**: Configuring home/office Wi-Fi, setting up firewalls, and securing your local network. (Price: $75/hour)
3. **Data Recovery**: Attempting to recover lost files from corrupted hard drives or USBs. (Price: $100 flat fee + $20/hour)
4. **Cloud Migration**: Helping businesses move their local data and applications to AWS or Google Cloud. (Price: Custom quote, starting at $200)
